Tips based on your points

Keep up the good work!

  • Embrace the Joy of Missing Out (JOMO) by actively celebrating the peace and mental clarity you gain when you choose not to know every piece of internet news.
  • Share your goals with a friend or partner and ask them to gently call you out if they notice you slipping back into old phone habits.
  • Create a digital detox calendar where you schedule one full weekend or 24-hour period every month to go completely offline.

You can do better!

  • Use hard-stop reminders like setting a loud, annoying alarm for 15 minutes right before you open a social media app. When it goes off, it forces you out of the scrolling trance.
  • Downgrade your device temporarily by using an old "dumbphone" (flip phone) on weekends to completely disconnect while remaining reachable for emergencies.
  • Use the "Out of Sight" rule by placing your phone in a drawer, a zipper pocket in your backpack, or even another room. If you have to stand up to get it, you will stop checking it automatically.

Find your balance!
