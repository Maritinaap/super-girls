What is Digital Well-being?

Digital wellbeing is basically how healthy your relationship with technology is. It’s not about deleting every app or becoming a phone-free monk — it’s about finding a balance. It means being aware of how much you use technology, what you use it for, how it affects your sleep and focus, how social media makes you feel, and how safely you behave online. In short: technology should work for you, not the other way around.

But could you use a digital reset?

Yes if...

  • You feel anxious, stressed, or depressed when you’re online or after using your phone.
  • You have trouble sleeping because you’re on your phone late at night.
  • You feel like you can’t focus on anything for long.
  • You feel like you’re addicted to social media.
